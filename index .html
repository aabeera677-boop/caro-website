About Our Company

CARO Technologies is an early-stage technology project exploring innovative translation tools. Our goal is to make communication easier between people from different countries and cultures.

Our Technology

We are developing the concept of smart translation earbuds and a companion mobile application.

🎧 Translation Earbuds

Future wireless earbuds designed to support two-way translated conversations.

📱 CARO Mobile App

A planned Android and iPhone application for text and voice translation.

🌍 Multiple Languages

Our language goals include Urdu, English, Korean, Japanese, Arabic and Chinese.

📷 Camera Translation

A planned feature for translating text from photographs.

Our Vision

We imagine a world where language does not stop people from making friends, learning, travelling or working together.
